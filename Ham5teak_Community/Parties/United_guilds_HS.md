# The United Guilds of Ham5teak

***Written by Discovery1031A***

The United Guilds of Ham5teak is an organization formed during the early months of Ham5teak Network for upholding the peace within the server. Started by Miner_Puffy and his guild Decay, eight member guilds have signed the Declaration of Formation.

## The guilds involved:

### The Union of Ham5teak Survival Republics (The H5 Union)
**Date of Formation:** 12th of December 2021

Formed during the height of the original Ham5teak by Discovery1031A, HandsomeshadowYT, and ItsEdGianPlayz 3 days before New Years 2022, the original doctrine of the Union was to "defend Ham5teak with all means necessary", regardless if there were rules broken or not. In 2023, two members of the Union (HandsomeshadowYT and Discovery1031A) had Staff positions in CloudLands Network, and HandsomeshadowYT would be instated as President of the Union. Discovery1031A would lose his Jr. Mod position 2 months after his acceptance into the position. By 2023, the Union have a small presence within CyperLands Network, with Discovery1031A leading the Union as President while HandsomeshadowYT went on hiatus. Subsequently, the last weeks of October 2024 was the prime show of power of the Union as Discovery1031A led the charge during the destruction event of CyperLands Season 07 alongside SammyDaKing21 (now JohnPKetchup), Inflamed (now EtherealMist_), Jozeph345, SenpaiPlayz, Cyvertray_, DCS_GAMER (now RagedBoltz), OcetdTheIX (for potions support), UKFIGHTER, among others.

By 2025, the Union's doctrine changed from war to peace, with some defensive initiatives included. Discovery1031A publicly announced that the Union would stand in solidarity with Starnix Network, and that solidarity would persist for 11 months, while celebrating their 4th Anniversary in Starnix Season 01. From March 3 to July 2026, the period of Starnix Season 02, major projects were successfully done by the Union over the course of four months, and one of their members would voice some of the server's YouTube Shorts videos. They are currently using the same doctrine of peace in Ham5teak Network since July 13, 2026.

**Out of all of the guilds in the United Guilds of Ham5teak, the H5 Union is the oldest, with them celebrating their 5th Anniversary in 4 months time.**
