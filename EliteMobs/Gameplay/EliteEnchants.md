# Elite Enchants 
 
 In elitemobs, the players can get access to special custom enchants not available through vanilla enchanting ( and for the most part, not included in AE's either). These offer special effects against elite mobs, and can be gotten through procedurally generated loot that is dropped from dungeons. 











	## Critical Strikes

Critical Strikes give you a chance to deal 1.5x damage when fighting Elite Mobs. Each level increases your critical hit chance by 10%.

| Attribute | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Effect** | Deals 1.5x damage to Elite Mobs on a critical hit |
| **Chance per Level** | Level 1 = 10%, Level 2 = 20%, Level 3 = 30% |
| **Maximum Level** | Level 3 |
| **Global Cap** | 40% maximum chance across all worn gear |

### How the Cap Works

Your total critical strike chance combines across all 6 equipment slots: helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, main hand, and offhand. 

However, your total chance is capped at **40%**. Once your combined gear reaches this limit, adding more Critical Strike levels to your remaining items provides no extra benefit.

## Drilling

The Drilling enchantment allows your tools to clear multiple matching blocks at once in an expanded radius. It only mines blocks of the exact same type as the targeted block. 

> **Tip:** Sneak while mining to temporarily disable the area effect and mine single blocks normally.

| Attribute | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Effect** | Mines matching blocks in an expanded radius |
| **Applicable Tools** | Pickaxes and Shovels |
| **Level Range** | Levels 1 through 4 |

### Mining Area by Level

* **Level 1:** Mines 1 extra block straight ahead.
* **Level 2:** Mines a 4-block cross pattern around the target block.
* **Level 3+:** Mines stacked 3x3 layers extending deeper with higher levels.

| **Maximum Level** | Level 2 |

## Loud Strikes

Loud Strikes is a dedicated tank enchantment designed to generate extra threat, forcing nearby Elite Mobs to focus their attacks on you instead of your teammates.

| Attribute | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Effect** | Multiplies threat generated when dealing damage |
| **Bonus Calculation** | Total Threat = Damage × (1 + combined bonus) |
| **Maximum Level** | Level 10 |

### How Threat Scaling Works
Each piece of gear adds its level divided by 3 to your total threat multiplier. Equipping multiple items with Loud Strikes stacks your threat across all 6 gear slots to lock mob aggression on you.

## Plasma Boots

Plasma Boots release an explosive ring of energetic particle projectiles whenever you jump, damaging nearby enemies on impact.

| Attribute | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Effect** | Releases damaging particle rings upon jumping |
| **Applicable Armor** | Boots |
| **Damage Output** | 2 damage per ring hit over a 3-second duration |
| **Maximum Level** | Level 3 |
| **Cooldown** | 2 minutes |
